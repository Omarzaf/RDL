Data Notes

Publication caveats

  • Temporal data: firm sizes reflect aggregate spend repeated across years, not observed year-specific values.
  • Coverage: the initial scene shows a capped high-signal subset for browser performance.
  • Confidence: medium and low confidence entities have incomplete evidence or inferred placement.
  • 2025 data is partial. 2026 is not included in the visual payload.
  • Revolving-door counts represent resolved career-transition records; duplicate-person controls are applied upstream where person identifiers are available.
  • Revolving-door arc peaks and center points are visual curve controls only. They do not indicate a physical agency, office, or hidden hub.
  • Unknown revolving-door transition years are kept unknown and are shown only in the selected context, not echoed across every year.
  • Relationships may be capped for performance.
  • Government nodes use lobbying exposure directed at the agency, not agency spending.
